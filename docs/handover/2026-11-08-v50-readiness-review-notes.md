# v50.1 readiness-review notes (started 8 Nov 2026)

Items the architect has sent to the readiness review. Add to this list; do not close an item without a recorded ruling.

1. **Rollback v50 -> v49 during a boot storm (residual; architect, 8 Nov 2026).** The chip's external watchdog stays armed across a flash. v49's boot-storm holdoff sleep (six early resets, a 600 s ULP sleep) runs before v49's first watchdog service: in `daec561` the storm sleep is at `Generalized-Core-Counter.cpp:~396-405` and the vendored `setWDT` at `:811`, the first loop service at `~:1176` (`lib/AB1805_RK/AB1805_RK.cpp:65-70`). If a v49 image boots into a retained boot-storm state while the chip dog is still armed, the dog can reset the board within 124 s and cut the holdoff short. This is **pre-existing in v49's structure** (found by Codex in the integrated Stage 7 round 1, Finding 2 rollback paragraph), not introduced by v50, and **not a bench case**: bench item 2 covers the normal rollback only. v50.1 itself closes the same hole on its own boot path (round-1 Finding 1: the external watchdog is initialised right after the bind and stopped/started around the storm sleep).
2. **The stale evidence sentinel (accepted residual; architect, 8 Nov 2026).** A stale 0xA5 can mislabel at most one later reason-20 classification as a power-up wake (a flush that fails, a reset between the write and the clear, or a rollback through v49, which ignores the byte); it is cleared at the next completed v50 boot. A complete fix would need a v49.1 change; out of scope.
