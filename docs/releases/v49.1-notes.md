## What changed since v48 (`v48-OccupancyEndsAtClose`)
- **Default time zone Eastern** (item 25, #176): a fresh device now runs the product 42131 default (`EST5EDT,M3.2.0/2:00:00,M11.1.0/2:00:00`) from first boot instead of UTC until a ledger zone is applied. A ledger time-zone change still takes effect at the next reboot.
- **Durable deliberate resets** (item 26, #181): occupancy and daily totals, the open session, alert codes, system status, sensor settings and the publish queue are flushed before a deliberate reset or power-down (thrash tier 3, Error soft/hard, nightly heap guard, sleep exhausted, failsafe stages 2 and 3). Known limitation: at most one in-flight publish event can be lost per deliberate reset.
- **USB charge-latch recovery** (item 22, #172): one charging cycle after 2 h stuck at "Not Charging". [IN: pending #172 merge.] Known limitation: alert 22 may clear early after a real charge fault and can mask alert 20.
- **RTC binding guard** (WO-2026-11-04-002): any stored Device OS RTC binding is deleted at boot (one reset with data 8 if one was found), so a later native-driver build can always roll back to this version. No effect on a device without one (the whole fleet today).

## Not in this release
- Untrusted-clock session fixes (item 19 C1/C2) and the AB1805 native-driver switch: v50.

Previous default: v48 (re-pin in the Console). Once v50 ships, v49 is the rollback floor.
