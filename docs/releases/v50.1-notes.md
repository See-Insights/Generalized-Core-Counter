## What changed since v49.1 (4901)
- **The AM1805 runs on Device OS's built-in driver** (v50 S1 #195, S1b #196, integrated fixes #208): boot, configuration and time move off the vendored library; the RTC is seeded before the Device OS binding.
- **Sleep alarm, external watchdog and wake classification on Device OS** (S2 #201): the chip watchdog is armed and fed through its register (#237: the earlier build passed `.watchdogPin(D5)`, so the chip reset the device about 124 s after every wake; fixed and bench-proven), and a failed feed is logged.
- **Timed power-off for the last-resort stages** (S3 #204): stage 3 and the error-state hard recovery cut power for 30 s through the chip. Proven on USB (19 Nov) and on battery only (20 Nov, Dev-14): the board comes back with reset reason 40.
- **Oscillator-fault latch** (S4 #205).

## Not in this release
- The connect decision with a hold after a failed connect (PR #244), the boot-storm detector counting PANIC and UNKNOWN (#221), and clock trust surviving a restart (#214): v50.2.
- Occupancy debounce default (#232) and alert reporting P (#231): held until RC1.

## Known issues (unchanged from v49.1)
- **Back-to-back connect retries when the radio cannot attach:** fixed in v50.2 (#244).
- **A stale cloud session after about 69 h of uptime** on one AT&T unit (ChurchSt-1a): the report connects but is not delivered until the 3 h failsafe reset clears it. Under investigation.

## Rollback
Previous default: v49.1 (4901; re-pin in the Console). v49.1 removes any stored Device OS RTC binding at boot, so the rollback is clean.
