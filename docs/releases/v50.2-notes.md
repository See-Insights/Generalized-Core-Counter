## What changed since v50.1 (5001)
- **Connect hold after a failed connect** (#244): one connect decision replaces the scattered retry rules. After a connect fails, the device holds off retrying (1 h, doubling per failure up to 6 h) instead of trying again at every report; only a service request connects inside the hold. It logs `CONNECT_HOLD: remaining=... failures=... trigger=...`. This fixes the back-to-back retries when the radio cannot attach.
- **Boot-storm detector counts PANIC and UNKNOWN resets** (#221): a run of crash or unknown resets now trips the detector.
- **Clock trust survives a restart** (#214): a restored clock is carried across a reset when it agrees with the RTC within 60 s and is cleared at 61 s or more; the logged correction is no longer near zero.

## Not in this release
- Occupancy debounce default (#232) and alert reporting P (#231): held until after RC1.

## Known issues
- **Alert 44 (ledger sync timeout before sleep) is tracked, not gating:** 6 events on 5 of the 11 units on 5001 in about 34 h (24 to 25 Nov), no reset with any of them. Ledger timelines were captured for the investigation.
- **Failsafe backlog:** a stale cloud session after about 69 h of uptime on one AT&T unit (ChurchSt-1a) still waits for the failsafe reset (it recurred on 25 Nov: alert 40 at 11:00Z, reset 140/2 at 14:10Z, back at 15:11Z); the staged cellular-acquire recovery is still cloud-only.
