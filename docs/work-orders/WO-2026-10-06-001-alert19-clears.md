## Background (Claude Code's read-only lookup, 2026-10-06)

- **What it is:** alert 19 means "watchdog reset": the MCU watchdog (reason 60), or an AB1805 watchdog confirmed on a pin reset. `setup()` raises it at `src/Generalized-Core-Counter.cpp:1305` with severity 4, the highest (`src/MyPersistentData.cpp:876–877`). It was added in `75ad0a8` (2026-08-10).
- **Sticky by design until now:** the comment at `:1300–1304` says so. It isn't in `isAutoClearAfterReportAlert()` (`:1930–1941`), and every other clear site is limited to its own codes.
- **It survives reboots:** it's persisted in `/usr/current.dat` (`MyPersistentData.h:684`).
- **It masks every other alert:** `raiseAlert()` only replaces a less severe code (`MyPersistentData.cpp:900–902`).
- **Fleet impact (latest status per device, 6 Oct):** 10 of 11 devices show alert 19, with `lastAlert` from 12 Aug to 4 Oct. Alerts 15, 40 and 41 have been hidden behind it. Example: Dev-09's alert 15 on 5 Oct logged as `alert=19`.
- **Court2 is the exception, at 0.** Its only two watchdog resets were under `21.0_Test`, and its status at both shows alert 0.
- **What the fields are:** the report's `alerts` and the status `alert` both carry the **current code, not a count** (`:1977`, written at `:2007`/`:2026`; status `:2150`).
- **Why clearing loses no forensics:** `watchdogResetCount`, the `lastWatchdog*` fields and the `watchdog` event keep the history (`:1289–1309`).

## Change. Budget: +1 net `src/` line, plus one host test with a mutation

- `src/Generalized-Core-Counter.cpp:1930–1941`: add `case 19:` to `isAutoClearAfterReportAlert()`. The existing clear-after-report block (`:2058–2067`) then clears it once the first report carrying it is queued.

- Unchanged: the severity order. While 19 is set, it still outranks other alerts.
- **Accepted consequence:** an alert active when the watchdog fires (for example 41) is superseded by 19, as today, and isn't restored after 19 clears. A condition that persists raises its alert again.
