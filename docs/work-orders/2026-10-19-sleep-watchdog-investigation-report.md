## 7. Leading hypotheses, ranked by evidence (no fix proposals)
1. **A Device OS/modem stall inside or around `System.sleep()` (entry teardown or wake resume), over 60 s with no watchdog service.** For: bc28 is set immediately before `System.sleep()` and the app watchdog is 60 s (`AWAKE_WATCHDOG_TIMEOUT_MS`, ULP pause); `elapsed` 36–68 ms means no long app stage; both serial cases show a reset about 60 s after a wake with a silent app, and a failing modem right after (power-off failure; NCP no response); bench teardowns are chronically 19–29 s. Against: 15/19 US events had only connecttime ≤16 s reports before (standby apparently not suppressed, so the latch/modem-off path was not obviously engaged); no pairs within 30 min; 0 alert 15 (weak).
2. **Awake-watchdog pause/resume accounting across many short wake/sleep cycles (inference only).** For: 17/19 occupied, 6 with ≥200 wakes in 2 h, resets 60 s after a wake. Against: 10 events had <35 wakes; the two hourly-timer-wake events; the per-sleep rate looks uniform (85% of sleeps in busy hours vs 89% of events occupied).
3. **Coincident cell/cloud trouble.** For: PCKL1 outage overlapping PCKL2 on 12 Oct, Court3 blackout around Court1 on 4 Oct, PCKL1 connecttime 211 on 14 Oct, PCKL over-representation (p about 0.05), PCKL3/PCKL1 delays on 4–5 Oct. Against: nothing in the prior 2 h for about 8 of 19, and no pair within 30 min.
4. **Queue/ledger work at sleep entry.** Queue 0 in 16/19; alert 44 only after resets (PCKL1 ×2); weakest.

**Discriminators:** (a) a timestamped log of the 60 s before a reset (the last line before silence, and whether a `Wake:`/PowerDiag post-wake line appears) from a device with serial. Only the two bench devices have one, and 2 of 2 existing cases are wake-side; the next bench event on 6.6.0 decides entry side vs wake side. (b) For PCKL (11/18 events, all 5 recent), cell-side data at the estimated reset times (Particle device vitals, registration changes, and whether the modem was in standby at the sleep call) versus the same hour on non-event days.
