- **Root cause.** The occupancy debounce had **several copies, in two units**: milliseconds (`setting1`, the helper `Config::occupancyDebounceMsForRuntime()`, `Config::DEFAULT_OCCUPANCY_DEBOUNCE_MS`) and whole seconds (the awake PIR branches at `State_Modes.cpp:76-103`, the occupied sleep plan at `State_Sleep.cpp:611-618`), plus a second fallback owner in the wrapper `State_Common.h:322-326`.
