## 2. The night case: acceptable, and it cannot loop

- **When can an Unknown clock act at night?** `todayAt(openTime)` is today's local 06:00. **22:00 to 24:00:** that is this morning (past), so `ageBase = max(06:00, lastConnection)` and a device with no connection for 3 h can fire stage 2 (the age crosses 3 h at about 09:00; at 22:00 it already has). **00:00 to 06:00:** the local date has rolled, `todayAt(06:00)` is in the future, so `now <= ageBase` returns: **no action.** So the new night exposure is only 22:00–24:00 local, and only for an Unknown clock with no connection for 3 h or more (a broken device in any hour).
- **No loop:** the stage is persisted before the reset (`persistConnectivityFailsafeState(2, now, true)`, `:302`), so after the reboot `currentStage = 2`, `nextStage = 3`, and `currentStage != 0 && lastAction != 0 && (now - lastAction) < COOLDOWN (6 h) + jitter (up to 30 min)` returns (`:260`). Stage 2 cannot repeat; stage 3 needs 6 h; at stage ≥ 3 the supervisor returns until `Failsafe: cleared reason=cloud-ok`. (A `lastAction > now` clock glitch zeroes `lastAction` and so skips the cooldown, but that advances to stage 3, not back to 2.) The STOP condition is not met.

## 3. What the fix does for Dev-09 (so the architect knows what it buys)

With Unknown treated as Open, Dev-09 (stage 2 at 23 Oct 10:40:40Z, never connected afterwards): **22:00–24:00 SGT (14:00–16:00Z)** the 6 h cooldown (to 16:40Z plus jitter) has not elapsed, so no stage 3; **00:00–06:00 SGT** `now <= ageBase` returns; **06:00 SGT (22:00Z)** the age restarts from today's opening, and crosses 3 h at **01:00Z (09:00 SGT) on 24 Oct**, when stage 3 (the deep power-down) fires. So the fix replaces "never" by "about 9 h after the next open", **not** by a quick recovery. That is the WO-2026-10-02-001 consequence it recorded ("a late stage 2 pushes stage 3 into the next open period"). Chip's manual reset at 00:53Z was 7 min earlier than that moment and the device then connected after 193 s. Shortening this (the age base, the cooldown) is a separate decision and is **not** in this WO.
